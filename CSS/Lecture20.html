Meet Jerry

Jerry, a scruffy terrier mix with mismatched socks, has a peculiar habit of hoarding shiny objects. His stash, hidden beneath the living room rug, includes bottle caps, lost buttons, and even a sparkly earring he pilfered from his owner's dresser. Despite his kleptomaniac tendencies, Jerry is a loyal companion, greeting his family with enthusiastic tail wags and sloppy kisses. His favorite pastime is chasing squirrels in the park, though he's never actually caught one!

Meet Tom

Tom, a sleek black Bombay cat with piercing green eyes, is a mischievous three-year-old. He's a charming mix of aloofness and affection, pretending to be independent while secretly craving cuddles. Tom has a strange obsession with bottle caps, batting them around the house like a hockey puck. While his favorite food is tuna, he'll try to steal any unattended food. He prefers to nap in sunbeams or atop the warmest electronic device.
